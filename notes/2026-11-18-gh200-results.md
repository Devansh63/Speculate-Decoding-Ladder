# GH200 results, 18 Nov 2026: what the dtai2 pieces show

All 43 pieces of the c1 and conc bundles ran (jobs 3618562 to 3618604, 21 of
them through ghx4-interactive by `dtai2_feeder.sh`), plus the earlier smoke,
k0/pre, profile and trial jobs. Results: `results/gh200/` (commit 4018935).
The domain bundles have not run yet. Everything below the line is generated by
`python3 code/analyze_gh200.py results/gh200`; rerun it when more results land.

## Summary

1. **Clean data.** 262 rows, all on Model Runner V2 with async scheduling,
   harness v5c, no warm-up prompt measured, per-request timing on every
   request. The only gap is by design: the three acc pieces have no off arm
   (vLLM 0.33.0 refuses per-request acceptance without a drafter,
   `vllm/config/vllm.py:1734`), so their baselines are the core off arms.
2. **Repeatable to well under 1%.** The same off arm in five jobs varies by
   0.14% (2K), 0.27% (8K), 0.74% (32K) sd/mean; the rep piece reproduces the
   core speedups within 0.5%, across different jobs and nodes. loopA (each
   prompt timed alone) agrees with core to 0.01x on every arm but one
   (2K n-gram k=1: 0.99x against 1.12x).
3. **Length decides the drafter.** At c=1, DFlash k=15 gives 2.10x at 2K and
   1.67x at 8K; at 32K it falls to 1.11x and GPU n-gram wins (1.19x at k=7).
   DFlash k=15 acceptance at position 1 drops from 0.65 (2K) to 0.55 (8K) to 0.32
   (32K). The best DFlash k is 15 at every length; n-gram peaks at k=11 (2K,
   8K) and k=7 (32K).
4. **Prefill dilution, as on the A40.** Prefill is 4% of the off run at 2K,
   14% at 8K and 44% at 32K (one-token cell and per-request split agree), and
   no drafter shortens it. Carrying an idle DFlash drafter costs 3.1% (2K),
   2.6% (8K), 2.4% (32K). Prefix caching makes no difference at 32K.
5. **Concurrency decides k.** At 2K, DFlash k=15 is best up to c=8 but drops
   to 0.97x at c=32, where k=4 gives 1.19x; GPU n-gram k=5 also falls below
   1x at c=32. At 32K every arm is near 1x by c=8 and n-gram k=5 stays best.
6. **Router headroom.** Choosing the arm by length and concurrency beats the
   best single arm (DFlash k=15) by 2 to 6% at 32K, 12% at 2K c=16 and 22.5%
   at 2K c=32, and by nothing at 2K and 8K for c up to 8. Within one length at
   c=1, a per-prompt oracle adds only 2.0% (2K), 2.7% (8K) and 5.0% (32K) over
   the best fixed arm, and a domain router (in-sample, 1 to 19 prompts per
   domain) recovers a quarter (2K) to two thirds (32K) of that. So the value is in reading length and
   load, not prompt content, on this workload.
7. **Not lossless on every arm.** DFlash k=1, DFlash k=15 and GPU n-gram k=11
   change the output of 72 to 92% of prompts against off, sometimes from the
   first token; every other arm (k=2, 4, 7, 11 DFlash; k=1 to 7 n-gram)
   reproduces off exactly. The output length is fixed at 256 tokens, so the
   timings still compare, but acceptance on those three arms is measured on
   different text. Open: which configuration difference (likely kernel or
   graph shapes) changes the target's numerics there.
