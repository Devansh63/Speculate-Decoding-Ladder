# Cost model, 18 Nov 2026: two constants explain the GH200 grid

Written from `results/gh200/` at commit 3906586 (all 43 dtai2 pieces plus the
2K code-completion and summarization task pieces, jobs 3627816 and 3627817).
Everything below the line is generated by `python3 code/cost_model.py`; rerun
it when the remaining task pieces land.

## Summary

1. **One number per drafter.** A speculative step yields tau tokens and costs
   R plain decode steps; prefill is untouched. So
   `speedup = 1 / (p + (1 - p) * R / tau)`, with p the measured prefill share.
   Backing R out of all 33 batch-1 QA arms gives **R = 1.25 for DFlash
   (1.23 to 1.27) and 1.02 for GPU n-gram (1.01 to 1.04)**, at every length.
2. **It predicts what it never saw.** Fitted on QA only, the two constants
   predict the 8 task-type arms (2K code completion, 2K summarization) and the
   4 output-length arms (32K, 64 and 512 tokens) from their own tau and
   prefill share: **mean absolute error 1.2%, worst 3.6%** (DFlash k=15 on
   code completion, 2.90x predicted against 2.79x).
3. **The winner rule.** At equal prefill share DFlash wins exactly when its
   tau exceeds R(DFlash) / R(n-gram) = **1.22 times** n-gram's. Measured: 2.15
   at 2K (QA), 2.15 (code completion), 2.18 (summarization), 1.73 at 8K, 1.07
   at 32K. That is why task type moved the 2K speedup (DFlash 1.91x to 2.79x)
   but not the winner: both drafters' acceptance moved together.
4. **Load raises cost, not acceptance.** At 2K, DFlash k=15's tau stays at
   2.78 to 2.83 from 1 to 32 concurrent requests while its effective R rises
   from 1.28 to 2.89; k=4 rises 1.25 to 1.84. That is why the best k shrinks
   under load. Under load prefill and decode overlap, so this R is an effective
   cost, not a clean per-step one.
5. **This updates C9, C12 and C13 for the GH200.** On the A40, DFlash's fixed
   drafting cost grew 2.24x from 2K to 32K (C9) and n-gram's 1.96x (C12). On
   the GH200 with vLLM 0.33.0 and Model Runner V2, neither drafter's R moves
   with length. The 32K inversion here is acceptance (DFlash's tau 2.80 to
   1.53) plus prefill dilution (4% to 44%), with no cost channel at all. The
   A40 measurements stand; the platforms differ (A40 vs GH200, vLLM version,
   model runner, CPU vs GPU n-gram). See `paper/claims.md`, 18 Nov.

## Why tau needs no coverage term here

On the A40, n-gram drafted on only 13 to 21% of steps, so its tau had to be
corrected for coverage (C7). On the GH200 with Model Runner V2, both drafters
draft on every step: draft steps x tau equals the tokens generated to within
0.5% on every arm (section 1). So tau is tokens per verify step and R is the
same R as in `HANDOFF.md`.

## Limits

- One model (Qwen3-8B), one GPU, greedy, 256-token outputs except section 3's
  output-length cells.
- The fit is a geometric mean; DFlash's R rises slightly with k (1.24 at k=1
  to 1.27 at k=15), so a per-k fit would tighten the DFlash predictions.
- The held-out test covers two tasks at one length. The queued 8K and few-shot
  and multi-document QA pieces are the next test; code editing (InstructCoder)
  is the copy-heavy case most likely to push the ratio below 1.22.
- No intervals yet.
