# C20 amended: the machine factor conflates two channels

Written 13 Oct 2026, hours after C20 was registered, following an external
review of the day's commits. Amends `paper/claims.md` C20. The conclusion
survives and strengthens; two supporting statements are withdrawn.

## The defect

This project defines `R` by the identity

```
R = tau_eff / speedup
```

so the "machine factor" as registered is

```
factor = R_A100 / R_A40 = (tau_A100 / tau_A40) / (speedup_A100 / speedup_A40)
```

**The factor therefore contains the acceptance ratio, not only the cost ratio.**
Threat 9 (same day) established that tau does *not* transfer between machines —
21 of 25 sequences differ between A40 and A100 — so the tau term is not a small
correction that can be waved through. It is the same channel C13's correction
(11 Oct) said must not be dropped, reappearing on the other side of the equation.

A tau-free factor holds acceptance fixed and asks what the cost ratio alone
would be: `speedup_A40 / speedup_A100`.

| cell | speedup A40 | speedup A100 | factor as registered | tau-free factor | d_tau |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2K n-gram k=5 | 1.1544 | 1.0587 | 1.0455 | **1.0905** | −4.12% |
| 2K DFlash k=7 | 1.8640 | 1.7860 | 1.0556 | **1.0437** | +1.14% |
| 8K n-gram k=5 | 1.1441 | 1.0789 | 1.0715 | 1.0605 | +1.04% |
| 8K DFlash k=7 | 1.2604 | 1.1054 | 1.1398 | 1.1402 | −0.04% |
| 32K n-gram k=5 | 1.0778 | 0.9744 | 1.0703 | 1.1061 | −3.24% |
| 32K DFlash k=7 | 0.8259 | 0.6432 | 1.2964 | 1.2840 | +0.97% |

The identity `registered = tau-free × (1 + d_tau)` holds to four decimal places
in every cell, so this is arithmetic rather than interpretation.

## Two statements withdrawn

**1. "At 2K the two rungs are indistinguishable (1.046 against 1.056)."
WITHDRAWN.**

| bucket | gap as registered | gap tau-free | tau-free ordering |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2K | 1.0 pt | **4.7 pts** | **n-gram higher** (1.0905 vs 1.0437) |
| 8K | 6.8 pts | 8.0 pts | DFlash higher |
| 32K | 22.6 pts | 17.8 pts | DFlash higher |

The rungs look indistinguishable at 2K only because n-gram's tau fell 4.12% on
A100 while DFlash's rose 1.14%, and those cancel a 4.7-point difference in the
cost term. On the tau-free metric the rungs differ at 2K by more than they
appear to differ as registered, **and in the opposite order**.

**2. The branch-B label. WITHDRAWN.** The 11 Oct prediction pre-registered
three diagnostics, the second being "a factor that differs between rungs at long
context but not short". C20 claimed that signature. On the tau-free metric the
rungs differ at 2K as well, with n-gram above DFlash, so the observed pattern is
neither branch B nor the "broader than B" reading proposed and then retracted
earlier the same day. **The pre-registered diagnostic list does not contain the
pattern that occurred**, which is a more interesting outcome than matching one
of its branches and should be recorded as such rather than forced into a label.

## What survives, and is stronger

C20's conclusion — **the machine factor is not a property of the machine** — is
untouched and better supported. As registered it rested on the rungs agreeing at
2K and diverging after. On the tau-free metric the rungs disagree at *every*
bucket, which is a stronger statement: there is no context at which a single
scalar relates A40 cost to A100 cost across rungs.

The `R_draft` fits are unaffected. They are fitted within one machine across k,
so no cross-machine tau ratio enters them: DFlash 0.299 / 0.445 / 0.673 and
n-gram 0.071 / – / 0.139 stand as measured, as does the finding that the fixed
drafting term dominates `c·k` by 4–7× and grows roughly twice as fast.

## The fix is not a different scalar

The tau-free factor is itself a **counterfactual** — it asks what the factor
would be if tau transferred, and threat 9 says it does not. Replacing one
contaminated scalar with one counterfactual scalar is not an improvement.

**Report the two channels as two columns and stop claiming a single machine
factor exists.** That is what C20's title already asserts and what its evidence
section then contradicted by quoting one number per cell. The scoreboard table
already carries the `d_tau` column, so the decomposition costs nothing.

**Consequence for the collapse figure.** The planned figure plots machine factor
against `R_draft`. With the factor as registered, the y-axis is contaminated by
acceptance while the x-axis is a pure within-machine cost fit — so the figure
would show a cost model against a quantity that is partly acceptance. Either
plot the tau-free factor and label it a counterfactual, or plot the speedup
ratio and the tau ratio as two series. **This must be settled before the figure
is drawn**, not after.

## Reproducibility note

The factor table is not reproducible as published without one line that was
omitted: **`R` is averaged over same-cell realizations on both sides** before
the ratio is taken. An external reviewer recomputing 8K n-gram obtained 1.0704
against the published 1.0715 by choosing a single A40 realization as the
reference. Both are defensible; only one is what the table reports. Any future
factor table states its aggregation.
