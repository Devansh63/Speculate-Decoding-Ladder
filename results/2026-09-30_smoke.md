**Date:** 30 Sep 2026, interactive session on gpub032. Purpose: prove the harness,
not to measure anything. No `off` baseline, so no speedup.

| rung | k | tok/s | mean accepted length | position 1 |

Truncated to a common k=5, DFlash gives 2.591 against n-gram's 2.585: level at
2K on five prompts. The 100-prompt run separates them.

**Engine startup cost measured here:** 263 s for the no-drafter case, 209 s with
DFlash, dominated by torch.compile (44 s) and JIT kernel warmup (115 to 136 s).
This is per process, so a nine-cell sweep pays it nine times. Hence the shared
compile cache at `$ROOT/.vllm_cache`.
